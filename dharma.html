Nusantara Dharma

Book Festival

The Nusantara Dharma Book Festival 2020 was Indonesia's most comprehensive online Buddhist and Nusantara book fair. It was the real-world outcome of the #bukabukubukamata and #bukabukubiarbahagia campaigns. The event spanned over two weeks and drew 1,367 online participants.

Scope of work:

  • Brand Guideline
  • Event support
  • Digital presence

Our team successfully published over 150 pieces of content, including event posters, book promotions, fun facts, and more.

We took the lead as the main coordinator for the Publication Division, managing all aspects related to content and publicity.

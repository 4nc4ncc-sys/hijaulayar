Cosmo

Cosmo is a pet photo and videography brand that captures authentic moments between pets and their humans.

Built around warmth, playfulness, and emotional connection, the brand focuses on turning simple interactions into meaningful visual stories.

Scope of Work

  • Brand Identity Development
  • Logo Design
  • Color Palette & Typography System
  • Visual feed & reels concepts

Goal:

Our goal was to create an identity that feels friendly, approachable, and full of personality just like the pets themselves.
